# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary readers are UK residents who have received a suspicious email, text, website, call or offer and need to make a safe, timely decision—often before sending money, sharing credentials or personal details, or installing unrequested software. Readers may also use the site's guides and research to understand scam patterns and recover after an incident.

## Product Purpose

Beat The Scam is an independent UK consumer-protection publication. It helps people recognise, check and respond to scams with plain-English guidance, a free AI scam checker, and original UK scam-statistics research. Success means a reader can take a safer next step quickly and find the appropriate official route for reporting or support.

## Positioning

The site combines practical, plain-English verification checks with an AI-assisted editorial workflow that validates reporting routes against a verified canon of official UK sources. It is an independent publication, not a regulator, bank, legal adviser or journalist-led newsroom.

## Operating Context

The site is used in moments of uncertainty and potential financial or privacy harm. Guides cover scam patterns and recovery actions; the checker helps readers assess suspicious material; research provides evidence about the UK scam landscape. Reporting and consumer-support routes must be correctly scoped to the reader's nation.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- 185 public scam guides, a free AI scam checker, and original UK scam-statistics research.
- The static site is generated by a bespoke Python build pipeline and served from committed `dist/` output on Netlify.
- The checker and newsletter use isolated Netlify Functions; the site has no user accounts, comments or direct purchases.
- Editorial content is AI-assisted and subject to an automated accuracy gate that validates official UK reporting routes against a verified canon.
- Content is educational and does not provide legal, regulatory or financial advice.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Beat The Scam.
- Voice: practical, plain-English, calm and safety-focused.
- Editorial independence must be clear: the site should not imply it is an official authority or regulated advice provider.
- The founder and editor is Alex Bacsa, a UK-based independent operator.

## Evidence on Hand

- Guide source records: `content/posts.json`.
- Verified reporting-route canon: `content/sources.json`.
- UK scam-statistics research dataset: `content/uk-scam-statistics.json`.
- Editorial methodology, accuracy controls and operational evidence: `docs/project.md` and `docs/next-session.md`.
- Do not fabricate testimonials, customers, regulatory endorsements, legal advice or performance claims.

## Product Principles

1. Put the safest practical next action within easy reach.
2. Translate scam and fraud risk into checks ordinary UK readers can understand quickly.
3. Treat accuracy in official reporting and support routes as a core product responsibility.
4. Be explicit about the publication's independent, educational role and its limits.
5. Preserve reader trust over engagement, conversion or advertising pressure.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Readers may arrive under stress, on mobile devices, and with varied digital confidence. The product should support clear language, keyboard access, visible focus states, responsive layouts and comprehensible actions without assuming specialist knowledge.
